Game files

S3R ships with no game content. It builds everything from your own copies of these games, so every item below is required. Point it at the folders that hold them; subfolders are searched too, and each item is recognised by what is in it.

The launcher only reads these files on your PC. It never uploads, shares or changes them.

Help

The questions people ask most.

What do I need?

Your own copies of Skate 3 with Title Update 3 and its 9 add-on packs, Skate 2 with its 3 add-on packs, Skate 1, and two PS3 mods: New San Van and Skate1Map. 18 items in all. The Game files page lists each one, and each has a "How do I get this?" guide.

Where are the add-on packs and the title update?

On the Xbox 360 they were downloaded to, in the hard drive's Content folder: under 454108E6 for Skate 3 and 4541087F for Skate 2. Copy those folders to your PC and add them on the Game files page.

How long does the build take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes, and only once. The launcher shows its estimate for your PC before you start, and you can keep using your PC. Most updates rebuild a few steps; the launcher says how long before you install one.

How much space do I need?

The launcher shows the space the build needs next to the free space on that drive before it starts, and stops early if there is not enough. Settings, Storage shows what the game uses and lets you clear leftovers.

My build stopped. What now?

Press Try again or Resume build. Finished steps are kept. If it stops at the same step again, press Save support file in Settings and attach it to a GitHub issue.

The game won't start.

Try Settings, Playing, Start in safe mode. If that works, a graphics setting was the problem, and your graphics are now back to default. If not, use Check and repair from the gear next to Play.

Is this made by EA?

No. S3R is an unofficial fan project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Electronic Arts, and you need your own copies of the games.
